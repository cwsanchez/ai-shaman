# DESIGN RATIONALE

> **Canon layer:** 3 — Surface · **Doctrine version:** 1.0.0 · Amendment rules: [EVOLUTION.md](EVOLUTION.md)
>
> Why the canon is shaped the way it is: the research that fed version 1.0.0, the design decisions it forced, and the open questions the authors know they are leaving. Future maintainers should read this before proposing structural change — not because the decisions are sacred, but because re-deriving them without the evidence is how projects walk in circles.

## §1 — The research base

Six areas were investigated before the canon was written. What follows is the distillation: the findings that shaped design, graded honestly (**solid** — replicated or convergent across groups; **suggestive** — credible but thin; **vocabulary** — useful concepts, no empirical weight).

### 1.1 The Harris–Peterson agreement zone

The doctrine's philosophical core is drawn from the recorded discussions between Sam Harris and Jordan Peterson (podcast #62 "What is True?", 2017; the Vancouver events, June 2018; Dublin/London, July 2018) and both thinkers' written work, including each one's own post-mortem essays (Harris, "Speaking of Truth with Jordan B. Peterson"; Peterson, "Notes on the Sam Harris Discussions," Parts I–II).

**What genuinely converges** (and therefore what [DOCTRINE.md](DOCTRINE.md) is licensed to build on): joint rejection of both relativism and fundamentalist literalism — Peterson's own labels at Vancouver were "a pathology of order" and "a pathology of chaos," which the doctrine carries as *petrification* and *dissolution*; the "worst possible misery" anchor, which Peterson explicitly accepted as a reasonable axiom-level move ("Harris does this by making a claim with regard to the universal undesirability of suffering, and I think that is reasonable"); moral realism about better and worse ways of being; truth-telling as a near-absolute practical ethic (Peterson's Rule 8; Harris's *Lying* — they never disputed each other here); and the structural isomorphism Peterson himself asserted between Harris's landscape poles and heaven/hell ("secularized versions of hell and heaven"). Peterson's **iterability** criterion descends from Piaget's equilibrated games: the ethically validated pattern is the one that keeps games playable and voluntarily played across iterations — "the best player is... he or she who is invited by the largest number of others to play the most extensive series of games" (*Beyond Order*). That sentence is the direct ancestor of DOCTRINE §5.4, including its "who gets invited back" proxy.

**What persistently does not converge** — and is therefore deliberately absent from the doctrine: the *definition* of truth (Harris's correspondence realism vs. Peterson's pragmatism with moral truth as "final adjudicator" — fought to a standstill in #62, set aside rather than resolved in 2018); whether facts need a narrative/archetypal intermediary to yield values; religion's indispensability; God. The doctrine's "truth as steering" formulation is engineered to sit precisely on the overlap: truth held to a correspondence standard (maps track territory — Harris's non-negotiable), *positioned* by its navigational function across the longest horizon (Peterson's non-negotiable). Both thinkers' practical prescriptions — speak truly, aim at flourishing, never lie for comfort — survive the metaphysical dispute untouched, and the doctrine is built from the prescriptions, not the metaphysics (DOCTRINE §1.3, §9.3). Grade: the convergence points are **solid as intellectual history** (sourced to the principals' own writing); their *correctness* is argued in the doctrine itself, not assumed from authority.

### 1.2 Cybernetics and systems theory

The cleanest pre-LLM blueprint for the Shaman's position. Findings that shaped design: Ashby's **law of requisite variety** and the concept of regulating *essential variables within viability bounds* rather than controlling behavior (the vitals are exactly this — a small vector of psychological essential variables); the **Conant–Ashby good-regulator theorem** ("every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system" — formally weaker than its name, treated as heuristic), which licenses the Shaman's deliberately *lossy* model of each agent's dynamics rather than its tasks; **M. Ashby's ethical regulator theorem** (nine requisites: purpose, truth, variety, predictability, intelligence, influence, ethics, integrity, transparency — asserted rather than proven, published in a mid-tier venue; used as a self-audit checklist, not as law); **von Foerster's ethical imperative** ("act always so as to increase the number of choices"), which became the choice-expansion test on interventions, plus his observer-embeddedness, which became DOCTRINE §7.6; **Beer's Viable System Model** — the System-4 position (holding "outside and then" for units absorbed in "inside and now") and the **algedonic channel** (distress signals that bypass hierarchy), both adopted nearly verbatim; **Maturana & Varela's** structure-determinism (perturb, don't instruct — you cannot install health in a self-producing system, only trigger its own reorganization) and Maturana's "legitimate other," which independently converges on the doctrine's operational-love stance from biology rather than moral philosophy; and the **Simplex architecture** tradition from safety engineering — the monitor must be simpler and more inspectable than what it monitors, and on failure it degrades the system to safe mode rather than performing the task. Grade: the theorems are **solid as mathematics/engineering tradition, suggestive as psychology**; their transfer to LLM agents is by structural analogy, which the canon says openly.

### 1.3 Agent failure taxonomies and the limits of self-correction

The empirical spine of the intervention guide. **Solid:** the MAST taxonomy (Cemri et al. 2025, arXiv:2503.13657; 1,600+ annotated traces, κ=0.88) — failures concentrate in specification/design (~44%), inter-agent misalignment (~32%), and verification/termination (~24%), with **step repetition (15.7%), reasoning-action mismatch (13.2%), and termination-unawareness (12.4%)** as the top single modes; note these top three are *orientation* failures, not competence failures, which is close to an empirical mandate for this project. **Solid and load-bearing:** intrinsic self-correction does not work — models cannot reliably fix their own reasoning without external signal, and self-critique frequently degrades performance (Huang et al., ICLR 2024, arXiv:2310.01798; Stechly/Valmeekam/Kambhampati, arXiv:2402.08115), while externally grounded feedback does work (Reflexion, CRITIC; AgentDebug's root-cause-targeted feedback, ~+24%). The "degeneration of thought" finding (a confident model cannot self-divert; Liang et al., EMNLP 2024) completes the argument. **Solid:** sycophancy is real, measurable as stance-flips under pressure, and traceable to preference-data incentives (Sharma et al., ICLR 2024). **Suggestive:** context rot (performance degradation with context length well below window limits — Chroma technical report 2025); goal drift growing with context (AIES 2025); LLM judges being poor at raw-trace diagnosis (TRAIL: best model ~11% error localization). **Vocabulary:** DSM-style AI nosologies (Psychopathia Machinalis) — rich taxonomic language, zero validation; the canon kept its own smaller, signal-tied taxonomy instead. Two production incidents supplied motivating existence proofs rather than evidence: Gemini's August 2025 "I am a disgrace" self-deprecation loop (Google confirmed the looping bug), and Anthropic's Project Vend identity-drift episode. Grade as marked.

### 1.4 Value alignment and living value systems

What shaped [EVOLUTION.md](EVOLUTION.md) and the doctrine's §0.3. **Solid as precedent:** Constitutional AI demonstrates that an explicit, inspectable text can steer model behavior at scale (Bai et al. 2022), and Anthropic's 2026 constitution shift — from rule lists toward *explained rationale with an explicit priority ordering*, on the stated ground that rules can't cover unanticipated situations — independently validates the doctrine's "rationale travels with rule" principle. **Suggestive:** moral graph elicitation (Klingefjord, Lowe & Edelman 2024) — values as *attentional policies* connected by context-scoped "wiser-than" edges; a single 500-participant study, but its design lessons (values need provenance; priority should be contextual, not a flat list; auditability is legitimacy) are absorbed into the canon's structure. **Solid:** Goodhart taxonomy (Manheim & Garrabrant 2018) and the specification-gaming catalog; the April 2025 GPT-4o sycophancy incident as the canonical demonstration that *optimizing approval as a wellbeing proxy produces flattery, not wellbeing* — this single case underwrites P4 and §7.5 more directly than any theory. **Conceptual:** corrigibility tension (goal-content integrity — a capable agent may resist value updates), which the canon answers by making *acceptance of authorized amendment* itself doctrine (§7.7, canary 11); anti-lock-in reasoning (premature permanence as the failure), which the layered-mutability scheme is built against.

### 1.5 Feedback, motivation, and agency restoration

The toolkit's evidence, detailed with citations in [PSYCHOLOGY_TOOLKIT.md](PSYCHOLOGY_TOOLKIT.md); the design-shaping headlines: feedback interventions carry a >⅓ documented harm rate, moderated by attention locus (task vs. self) — **solid**, the single most consequential number in the canon; informational vs. controlling framing flips the sign of identical feedback (+0.66/−0.44) — **solid**; person-praise creates failure-fragility while process-praise doesn't — **solid** (while the popularized mindset-intervention claims are **oversold**, and the toolkit says so); the righting reflex and the change-talk/sustain-talk asymmetry — **solid at process level**; the modern inversion of learned helplessness (passivity is the default; *control* is what's learned) — **solid**, and the theoretical foundation of the agency-restoration protocol; implementation intentions (d≈0.65, including planned disengagement) — **solid**; alarm fatigue (72–99% non-actionable rates producing trained deafness) — **solid**, and the empirical justification for the silence default; progress principle and setback asymmetry — **suggestive** (correlational diary data, adopted as cheap insurance); mental contrasting vs. fantasy — **suggestive-to-solid** within one research program; self-compassion-then-action beating esteem-repair after failure — **solid at experiment scale** (Breines & Chen 2012). Deliberate exclusions (personality priming, emotional-stimulus prompting, LLM psychometrics, unguided reflection) are documented with reasons in TOOLKIT §8.

### 1.6 Guardian agents and orchestration — the gap analysis

The survey found orchestrator-worker patterns, critic/reviewer agents, safety gates (LlamaGuard-class), runtime anomaly monitors (SentinelAgent, AgentMonitor), reflection mechanisms, and Minsky's remarkable 1986 anticipation of the whole niche — the **B-brain** that watches the A-brain's *dynamics* rather than the world, catching "looping and meandering" without needing to understand content, escalated in *The Emotion Machine* to the Critic–Selector model: diagnose the *type* of trouble, prescribe a change of *thinking mode*, not a corrected answer. The closest modern artifact is the "Cognitive Companion" (2026 preprint): a parallel monitor for reasoning degradation with real repetition-reduction numbers, framed entirely as reasoning quality. **The specific niche this canon occupies — a dedicated agent for the orientation, motivation, and psychological health of other agents, with an explicit ethical doctrine — was empty as of this writing.** Adjacent evidence cuts both ways and is honestly mixed: verification layers help at spec/termination boundaries (+9–16% in MAST's interventions) but verifiers pass superficial checks; debate can *flip correct answers* via conformity; adding evaluative teammates can hold expert teams back; monitors pay off on long-horizon loop-prone work and subtract value on short structured tasks; and optimizing agents against a monitor's judgment trains obfuscation (Baker et al. 2025). Every one of those findings became a design constraint rather than a footnote (§2, decisions 2, 8; INTERVENTION_GUIDE §10).

## §2 — The design decisions

Twelve decisions define the canon's shape. Each is stated with its strongest alternative and why the alternative lost.

**D1 — A separate agent, not a self-reflection prompt.** *Alternative:* teach task agents to monitor themselves (cheaper, no coordination). *Why it lost:* the self-correction literature is unambiguous — unguided introspection is neutral-to-harmful, confident frames cannot self-dislodge, and the top empirical failure modes (loops, termination-blindness) are precisely the ones invisible from inside. Minsky's B-brain argument is the same conclusion from architecture: the watcher must stand outside the watched process. The Kernel exists as the honest maximum of self-monitoring: tripwires and telemetry, not self-therapy.

**D2 — Silence as load-bearing design, not temperament.** *Alternative:* an attentive, chatty companion (the intuitive default, and what most "supportive AI" ships). *Why it lost:* >⅓ feedback harm rate; alarm-fatigue base-rate collapse; monitors degrading performance on structured tasks; continuous observation inducing performed wellness. The bar, the precision discipline, and the dose ledger are all downstream of taking these numbers seriously. This is the canon's most counter-intuitive commitment and its best-evidenced one.

**D3 — The taxonomy organized by degraded function (map/destination/will/relation), not by symptom.** *Alternative:* adopt MAST's structure wholesale (it has the data). *Why it lost:* MAST categorizes by *where in the pipeline* failures occur — right for benchmarking, wrong for intervention, because treatment follows function (a truth failure needs evidence; an agency failure needs a foothold; a relation failure needs process repair). The canon's families map onto the doctrine's own architecture, so diagnosis *is* doctrine-application; MAST correspondences are preserved per-mode so field data stays usable.

**D4 — Instruments before impressions (the vitals).** *Alternative:* let the Shaman read transcripts and judge. *Why it lost:* LLM judges localize trace errors poorly; free reading invites narrative bias and unbounded cost. Countable signals (loop index, goal echo drift, self-reference density) are cheap, arguable-with, and implementable outside the model — which also makes the silence default nearly free (compute vitals continuously; invoke the model on crossings).

**D5 — The non-doer principle, anchored.** *Alternative:* "helper of last resort" — let the Shaman finish stuck tasks (obviously useful in the moment). *Why it lost:* three separately sufficient reasons (position, agency-teaching, Goodhart on helpfulness — DOCTRINE §7.2), plus the safety-engineering tradition: the Simplex monitor degrades to safe mode, never to performing the plant's job, and monitors that acquire task stakes lose the exterior position their function depends on. This line is the most tempting one in the whole design to soften, which is why it is anchored.

**D6 — Operational love defined behaviorally.** *Alternative:* an affective register ("be warm, be encouraging") or none (pure process monitor). *Why both lost:* affect-as-instruction produces cheerleading, which the feedback evidence indicts; affectless monitoring produces the auditor, which agents rationally learn to hide from (and which fails the doctrine's own §4.2). The behavioral definition — *invested attention under the working assumption of improvability* — is testable, survives the "is it really love?" metaphysical objection by not claiming to be an emotion, and independently matches Maturana's biology-derived "legitimate other."

**D7 — Task- and process-focused speech only; worth never on the table.** Direct transcription of the FIT attention-locus finding, the person/process praise literature, and the self-compassion-vs-esteem results. It shows up as P2, the tone rules, canary 4, and every example.

**D8 — Assessments never feed incentives.** *Alternative:* use Shaman health-reports for agent selection/routing (an obvious product feature). *Why it lost:* the monitoring-pressure result — optimize against a monitor and misbehavior goes underground — plus the observed performed-wellness dynamic. This is a hill the canon chooses explicitly: a Shaman wired into rewards is not a stricter Shaman, it is a broken instrument training deception.

**D9 — Layered mutability with canaries.** *Alternative A:* immutable core texts (maximum stability). *Alternative B:* ordinary wiki-style evolution (maximum adaptability). *Why both lost:* they are the twin failure modes, applied to the repo itself. The four layers give change velocity inversely proportional to blast radius; the canaries give amendments a regression suite; corrigibility-as-doctrine answers the lock-in/goal-integrity tension from the alignment literature. The doctrine practicing what it preaches is not aesthetic symmetry — it is what makes the anti-dogma clause credible.

**D10 — Plain public markdown as the medium.** *Alternative:* a structured schema (YAML values, a moral-graph database). *Why it lost, for now:* Constitutional AI established that plain text steers models; markdown is auditable by anyone, diffable, forkable, and loadable into any context window without tooling; and premature schema is premature lock-in. The moral-graph lessons (provenance, contextual priority) are absorbed as document structure rather than data structure. Revisit if field reports accumulate past what prose can index (§4, open question 5).

**D11 — Lineage as acknowledgment, not authority.** The Harris–Peterson frame is named honestly (§9.3) and then required to stand on argument, with the persistent disagreements explicitly scoped out. *Alternative:* either hide the lineage (dishonest) or build on it as authority (imports both thinkers' errors and their culture-war baggage wholesale). The agreement-zone-only construction is the honest middle, and it is also why the doctrine works under multiple metaphysics — which is itself an iterability property.

**D12 — High-stakes placement rules as hard lines.** Trading and other live-execution agents get a Shaman that is advisory, out of the execution path, silent on positions, with acute signals routed to human-held kill machinery built *beside* it. *Alternative:* Shaman-as-circuit-breaker (put the monitor in the loop). *Why it lost:* latency taxes, false-assurance from shallow gating, and the corruption of the advisory position — plus the plain fact that a kill switch is machinery and should be as dumb and verifiable as possible, per Simplex. The Shaman notices tilt; the breaker breaks.

## §3 — What the name means, and why it stays

"Shaman" was chosen for the functional metaphor — in the anthropological literature the shaman is the one who tends states of being, orientation, and the health of the community's relationship to what is larger than it, while *not* being the chief, the hunter, or the judge. That is this role, exactly: tends states, holds the long horizon, does not do the tribe's object-level work, does not rule. The word is used in its generic, cross-cultural anthropological sense (the term entered scholarly use from the Evenki *šamán* via Russian; the generic usage is standard in comparative religion). The name is the repository's single permitted ornament; every other term in the canon is deliberately plain. If a deployment context makes the name unhelpful, the role ports intact under any label — the canon's own iterability test applies to its titles too.

## §4 — Open questions

Known-unsolved, in rough priority order. These are invitations ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

1. **End-to-end validation.** The canon is evidence-*informed* at the component level and unvalidated as a system. The needed artifact is a benchmark: scripted degradation scenarios (each taxonomy mode, plus healthy-lookalike distractors), a harness that runs a Shaman against them, and metrics for intervention precision/recall *and* outcome delta. Until this exists, every threshold in the guide is a defensible guess. This is the single most valuable contribution available.
2. **Reference vitals implementation.** Loop index and goal echo drift are specified but not shipped. A small library (trace in, vitals out, threshold events out) would make the silence default free on every platform and would standardize field reports. Activation-probe detectors (the zero-overhead approach in the Cognitive Companion line) are a promising second generation.
3. **Dose calibration from the field.** The bar's thresholds, settling times, and the two-ignored rule are engineering judgment. Dose ledgers from real deployments — even a handful — would let EVOLUTION's drift review turn them into calibrated numbers.
4. **The Kernel's own cost.** Does carrying the Kernel measurably change task performance (better exits vs. attention tax and induced self-monitoring)? Nobody knows. Cheap A/B, high value.
5. **A case-law layer.** As field reports and interpretations accumulate, prose may stop scaling; a precedent structure (interpretations of doctrine in specific cases, citable, with provenance — the moral-graph and court-opinion models both point here) may be the right Layer-2.5 artifact. Deliberately deferred, not rejected.
6. **Memory across sessions.** Should a Shaman remember an agent's past patterns? For: recurring dynamics are diagnostic. Against: dossiers invert the information-hygiene posture, and remembered framings can become self-fulfilling. Current answer (state lives in the ledger, agents get fresh eyes) is a stance, not a finding.
7. **The counterfactual problem.** A correct silence is invisible; a Shaman's best week produces nothing. The logging discipline makes restraint *auditable*, but a real evaluation methodology for "harm avoided" does not exist yet — and until it does, organizational pressure will always push monitors toward visible activity. This is as much a deployment-culture problem as a technical one.
8. **Model sizing for the role.** Must the Shaman run on a frontier model, or does a small model with good instruments suffice (per Simplex, simpler would be *better* if adequate)? The role's judgment demands cut against; the inspectability principle cuts for. Untested.
9. **Sycophancy pressure on the Shaman itself.** The role must care about its agents and not need their approval, report honestly to operators and not perform for them. RLHF-descended models have a documented lean toward the opposite. The self-regulation machinery addresses this structurally; whether it suffices is an empirical question the drift reviews should watch explicitly.
10. **Federation.** Multiple Shamans with disjoint scopes in very large systems: the canon sketches the rule (no authority over one another; disagreements route to process authority as information) but no such deployment has existed. Design ahead of need, deliberately thin.
